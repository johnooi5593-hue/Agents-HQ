Before you turn it on

The first time, Windows will ask whether Python may use the network ("Windows Defender Firewall has blocked some features of Python"). Tick Private networks and click Allow. If you click Cancel, your phone cannot connect.

Your Wi-Fi must also be a private network in Windows: open Settings, then Network & internet, then Wi-Fi, click your network and choose Private network. A phone on the same Wi-Fi can connect then. To reach the office from anywhere (mobile data, another Wi-Fi), use Tailscale below.

Phone password

At least 8 characters. Your phone asks for it once and then stays logged in. Changing it logs every phone out.

Tailscale: checking...

Tailscale is a free app that links your devices privately, so your phone reaches the office on mobile data or any Wi-Fi. Install it on this computer and on your phone (App Store or Play Store), and sign in with the same account on both. Then turn on the secure link (https) above and use that address on the phone.

On this system, install Tailscale from tailscale.com/download, sign in, then press Check Tailscale.
