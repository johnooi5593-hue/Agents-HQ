## Four separate editable designs

Use the inspected reference as visual evidence, with four meaningfully different directions rather than a collage or four crops of one canvas. Keep every option independently editable and exportable.

- **Cinematic:** controlled light, depth and restrained premium hierarchy, grounded in the reference.
- **Minimal:** deliberate space, precise alignment and strong readable type.
- **Energetic:** purposeful movement, contrast and dynamic scale without visual clutter.
- **Editorial:** intentional composition, expressive type hierarchy and disciplined texture.

These are directions, not permission to invent subject matter. Preserve meaningful source elements and the same identities across all options. Generate background imagery separately from final titles where possible; prefer real vector Chinese and English text with licensed, actually available fonts. Verify glyph rendering and spacing, especially Chinese punctuation and long titles. If custom lettering is preserved as pixels, disclose that it is movable/scalable imagery rather than editable characters. A flat background/subject remains raster even inside an editable SVG or HTML project; do not promise a layered PSD.

Retain source uploads, owned assets, project JSON, layer positions/fonts and immutable revisions. Save revisions without overwriting earlier exports. Restoring history creates a new current version. Editing a title must also update the project title fields so export validation checks the intended wording.
