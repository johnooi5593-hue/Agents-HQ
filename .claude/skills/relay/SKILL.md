# Relay: post copy, never the post itself

1. **Read** `notes.md` and `captions.srt` (or the transcript) in the clip's folder, and the brain (`mcp__memory__open_nodes` with names ["Company", "Brand rules", "Lessons"]). Write in the language of the video unless the brand rules say otherwise.
2. **Where.** One `post.md` per video that gets posted, next to it: `out/<name>/post.md`, or for a long talk cut into topics `out/<name>/<topic>/post.md` for each topic.
3. **Write** `post.md` in exactly this format. The app reads these headings and labels, so keep them as they are. Write **exactly 3 options** (different angles, not small rewordings). On the video's card in the app the owner picks one set, and that one option goes to Facebook, Instagram, YouTube **and TikTok** at once (owner, 2026-11-09: Instagram and TikTok are posted through Buffer with exactly the same caption), so each option must work on all four: within Instagram's limits (2,200 characters, at most 30 hashtags, hashtags inside the caption, no first-comment tricks). The picked `Title:` is the YouTube title, the text Codex designs the thumbnails from **and TikTok's title**, so it must read well big on a cover and stay under 60 characters. Each option: the `Title:` line, a caption whose first line works on its own, 2 to 3 short lines, then 3 to 5 hashtags. No file paths or notes to us inside an option. There is **no separate TikTok text** any more (an old `## TikTok` section is ignored by the app).

```
# <clip or topic name>

## Plan
- Post to: Facebook, YouTube Shorts (from Agents HQ); Instagram, TikTok (through Buffer, same caption, cover as frame 1)
- Best time: <day and time, from the lessons or the stats; "any time" if nothing is known>
- Why this works: <one line>

## Captions
### Option 1
Title: <YouTube + TikTok title, under 60 characters>
<first line works on its own>
<2 to 3 short lines>
<3 to 5 hashtags>
### Option 2
Title: …
…
### Option 3
Title: …
…

## Pinned comment
<one question that invites a real answer>
```

Only when one platform really needs different words, add a `## Facebook`, `## Instagram` or `## YouTube Shorts` section with the same `### Option N` numbering; it replaces that platform's text for the matching option. Instagram's words are always TikTok's words too, so an `## Instagram` override changes both.

4. **Check** every title and caption against the brand rules (length, no clickbait, names spelled right) and the Instagram limits above. Don't make covers or videos: Creative already made the one cover and the one video; the app itself makes the Buffer copy of the video (cover as frame 1) after approval.

End with: "Nothing is posted. Waiting for the owner's OK."
