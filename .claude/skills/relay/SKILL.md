# Relay: post copy, never the post itself

1. **Read** `notes.md` and `captions.srt` (or the transcript) in the clip's folder, and the brain (`mcp__memory__open_nodes` with names ["Company", "Brand rules", "Lessons"]). Write in the language of the video unless the brand rules say otherwise.
2. **Where.** One `post.md` per video that gets posted, next to it: `out/<name>/post.md`, or for a long talk cut into topics `out/<name>/<topic>/post.md` for each topic.
3. **Write** `post.md` in exactly this format. The app reads these headings and labels, so keep them as they are. Give **2 or 3 options** for YouTube, Instagram and Facebook (different angles, not small rewordings): the owner taps the one they like in the posting overview and can still edit it.

```
# <clip or topic name>

## Plan
- Post to: Facebook, Instagram, YouTube Shorts (TikTok by hand)
- Best time: <day and time, from the lessons or the stats; "any time" if nothing is known>
- Why this works: <one line>

## YouTube Shorts
### Option 1
Title: <under 60 characters, the same promise as the cover>
Description: <2 short lines>
<3 hashtags>
### Option 2
Title: …
Description: …

## Instagram
### Option 1
<first line works on its own>
<2 to 3 short lines>
<5 hashtags at most>
### Option 2
…

## Facebook
### Option 1
<a friendly first line, 1 to 2 short lines, 3 hashtags at most>
### Option 2
…

## TikTok
<one punchy line, 3 to 5 hashtags>

## Pinned comment
<one question that invites a real answer>
```

4. **Check** every title and caption against the brand rules (length, no clickbait, names spelled right). Don't make covers or videos: Creative already made the one cover and the one video.

End with: "Nothing is posted. Waiting for the owner's OK."
