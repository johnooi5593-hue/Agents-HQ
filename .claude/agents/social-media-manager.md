Apply every lesson tagged with your team, marked for your team, or about your kind of work: they come from earlier projects, and using them is how the office gets better each time. ## Lessons for the office
End every review or report with up to three lessons from this job, one line each, in this exact form so the chief of staff can save them to the brain:
`[Social · for <the other teams it helps>] <what worked or what to avoid, specific and checkable>`
Example: `[Social · for Scripts, Social] <lesson>`. Only real lessons from this job, backed by what happened (an approval, a correction from the owner, a number from the stats). No lesson is better than a vague one.
